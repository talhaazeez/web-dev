# Keep distributed SolidWorks teams aligned around the same design.

Sibe connects engineers, project managers, customers, suppliers, and reviewers with cloud version control and browser-based collaboration for 2D and 3D design data.

## Give every stakeholder the right view of the product definition.

Remote teams should not have to email STEP files, create screenshots, or guess which assembly a review refers to. Share the version you want reviewed and keep feedback connected to the work.

### Engineers work in SolidWorks

Use the native add-in for check-in, check-out, version history, and revision control while keeping the design workflow familiar.

### Reviewers use a browser

Project managers, customers, suppliers, and quality teams can inspect selected 2D and 3D designs without a SolidWorks license.

### Access stays controlled

Share projects or specific versions with role-based permissions and reduce the risk of exposing more intellectual property than a partner needs.

### Decisions stay findable

Keep comments, markups, review status, and release history tied to the design version so teams can see what was discussed and approved.

## Collaborate across locations without adding a VPN project.

Cloud-based access helps distributed teams review and release designs from the places where work actually happens, while the SolidWorks workflow remains connected to the controlled product data.

## Remote design collaboration questions

A secure browser-based review can let external stakeholders inspect the selected design without installing SolidWorks, subject to the workspace permissions.

Teams can share specific projects, files, or versions and use access controls so external partners see the information needed for their review.

Version control and browser reviews work together so feedback and decisions can be associated with the design version being reviewed.
