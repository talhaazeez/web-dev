## Repeated positioning and feature themes

- Cloud-based engineering collaboration platform purpose-built for SolidWorks teams.
- No server provisioning, no VPN, no expensive upgrades, no initial migration required, and quick setup.
- Native SolidWorks add-in for version control and revision workflows.
- Check-out/check-in for parts and subassemblies to prevent accidental overwrites.
- Version timelines with who changed what, when, and rollback to earlier versions.
- Approval states such as In Progress, Pending Approval, and Released; released revisions are locked.
- Browser-based review of 3D models and drawings for non-CAD stakeholders, suppliers, and customers.
- Secure sharing with role-based permissions and access revocation.
- BOM management, advanced search/metadata, reference management, and automated manufacturing exports.
- Free trial/no credit card/quick setup and free web viewers are recurring conversion themes.
