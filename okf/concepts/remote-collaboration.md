# Remote engineering collaboration for SolidWorks teams

Distributed teams can work around the same controlled design version while engineers continue in SolidWorks and project managers, customers, suppliers, quality teams, and reviewers use browser-based views. The public workflow emphasizes controlled access, comments, markups, and review history.
