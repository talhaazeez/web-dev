# SolidWorks BOM and product data management

The BOM workflow uses SolidWorks assembly structure and part metadata as a starting point for an indented bill of materials and downstream product data. Nested subassemblies, custom properties, versions, manufacturing exports, purchasing, and production planning are connected to the design context.
